Welcome

A quiet stretch of coastline, all to yourself

Costa Medellin sits along the shoreline of Medellin, a peaceful coastal town on the northern tip of Cebu Island. Surrounded by swaying palms and calm, clear water, the property offers a relaxed retreat away from the busier tourist spots of the island — just a few hours' drive from Cebu City.

Whether you're planning a family getaway, a small celebration, or a quiet stay by the sea, Costa Medellin is designed to feel like a home away from home, with direct beach access and space to unwind indoors and out.

Getting there

Medellin, Cebu

Located on the northern coast of Cebu Island, Medellin is known for its laid-back atmosphere and proximity to popular spots like Bantayan Island and Daanbantayan. It's roughly a 3-3.5 hour drive from Cebu City and Mactan-Cebu International Airport.

  • ~3.5 hrs from Mactan-Cebu International Airport (MCIA)
  • Close to ferry terminals for Bantayan & Malapascua Islands
  • Quiet, residential coastal barangay
